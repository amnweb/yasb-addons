# Control Center

Quick settings in one popup, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, do not disturb, microphone, volume, brightness, power plan and mode, and what is playing

```
yasbc addons install amnweb.controlcenter
```

A quick settings popup, like the one in Windows. Turn Bluetooth, Do not disturb and your microphone on or off, and set brightness, volume and microphone level. The ⌄ next to each slider picks the screen, speaker or microphone. Pick a power plan and power mode, control what's playing, and lock, sleep or shut down.
